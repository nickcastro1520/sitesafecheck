Privacy

Short version: SiteSafeCheck keeps as little as it can, for as short a time as it can, and sells nothing.

What we store

  • The domain you check and its report are cached for about 10 minutes so repeat checks are instant and the same site isn't scanned over and over.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address is kept for up to about a day to enforce fair-use limits. We don't store the IP address itself.
  • We don't ask for your name or email, and we don't use accounts.

Who sees what

  • Google Gemini: to write the plain-English explanations, the scanned domain and its findings are sent to Google's Gemini API. Nothing about you is included.
  • Google Analytics: if analytics is turned on, it counts visits and events like "scan started" and the grade a report got. The domain you check is not sent to analytics.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.
  • The site you check sees a handful of requests from our server identified as SiteSafeCheck/1.0. It doesn't see your IP address.

Contacting Nick

If you call, text, or email Nick from a report, that conversation is just between you and him. He uses it only to reply to you.
